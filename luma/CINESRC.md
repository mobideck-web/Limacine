# CineSrc playback

Luma uses the protected CineSrc embed. The experimental native module, gateway, HLS adapter and launcher are disconnected from the application. Its saved source is preserved separately in ../luma-cinesrc-archive and in the original reverse-engineering chat. No browser service is needed or started by Luma.

Saved titles, history keys, resume positions and Arabic subtitle defaults are unchanged. The retired /api/cinesrc/status endpoint returns enabled:false for compatibility. No resolution or media rewrite is configured.
